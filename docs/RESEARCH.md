# Notes on the original game

Collected from web sources; **verify against the sources before entering data**.
Sources: strategywiki.org (E.V.O.: Search for Eden, Evolutions + chapter pages), giantbomb.com wiki, flyingomelette.com review,
retroxp.beehiiv.com, almarsguides.com walkthrough, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.V.O.:_Search_for_Eden.

- Five chapters/eras; the player evolves through classes: fish -> amphibian -> reptile -> bird -> mammal.
- Chapter 1 is the age before land, played as a fish. Ocean chapters end with a shark boss (per one writeup).
- Eight upgradable body sections: jaws, horns, neck, body, hands and feet, dorsal fin, tail, back of head.
  The fish form starts with jaws, horns, body, hands and feet, dorsal fin and tail.
- Stats: max HP, attack types (bite, strength, kick, strike, horn), defence, agility, jumping.
- Economy: eating meat restores HP and gives EVO points (EP); plants heal only. EP buy minor evolutions.
- A major (story) class change wipes the minor evolutions and EP and starts at the basic creature of the new class.
- Crystals give hints, large EP rewards, or a temporary unique body.
- Death: no game over; Gaia revives you on the map with an EP penalty (one source says EP are halved).
- Finished levels can be revisited for more EP.
- Hidden Human form requires cat jaws, a rabbit body and several evolutions of the Ramapithecus body.
- Known weaknesses to fix in the remake: sloppy collision, bosses hitting much harder than normal enemies,
  evolving into forms that cannot heal during boss fights, grinding for EP.
- Final boss and ending are in Eden (details to verify).
